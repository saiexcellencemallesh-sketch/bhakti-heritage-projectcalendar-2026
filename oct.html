The Divine Mystic

Born in Udutadi, Akka Mahadevi (12th Century) was a visionary saint whose soul was wedded solely to Chenna Mallikarjuna (Shiva). Her beauty captivated King Kaushika, who forced her into marriage. However, Akka placed her devotion above royal duties.


When the King violated her conditions for worship, she engaged in the ultimate act of renunciation: she gave up her fine clothes and jewels, walking out of the palace covered only by her long hair. Wandering as an ascetic, she reached the Anubhava Mantapa, where she won the reverence of saints like Allama Prabhu with her spiritual brilliance.

The Srisailam Caves

Her journey culminated in the wild forests of Srisailam. In the solitude of the Kadali caves, she attained aikya (oneness) with the Divine.


Her living presence is felt there even today. In those very caves, water naturally drips onto the Shivalinga throughout the year, irrespective of the season—a timeless, miraculous testament to her unconquerable spirit and her eternal union with the Lord.
